Internal convention: unit tests follow Arrange-Act-Assert with one behavioral assertion per test; test names read as behavior sentences (methodName_condition_expectedOutcome).

Grading rubric for unit-test labs (0-4 each): coverage of the specified behaviors, boundary cases, readability of test names, and independence (no shared mutable fixtures).

Starter kit: each testing lab ships a small service with seeded bugs; learners must catch at least 4 of 6 seeded bugs with their test suite.
