\begin{abstract}
LLM-based agents for instructional systems design (ISD) optimize the quality of a single design pass: a fixed design brief goes in, and the best possible draft comes out. Real training programs in corporate settings, however, are living artifacts that must be refined continuously as deployment feedback arrives and technology changes. Existing architectures leave designers with a dilemma at every refinement request: regenerate the entire program, which is costly and discards previously validated work, or let the model edit locally without any control mechanism, which risks silently breaking the constructive alignment among objectives, activities, and assessments. We present \textbf{AlignmentGraph-ISD}, an AI agent harness that accompanies a training program through its full lifecycle---from brief elicitation, through initial generation, to post-deployment refinement sessions. Its central feature is the representation of the instructional design as an explicit, versioned alignment graph; around this graph, the harness organizes multi-agent orchestration, pedagogical verification, cascade-scoped revision, agentic retrieval-augmented generation, and multi-session memory, so that each refinement request regenerates only the affected portion of the design under alignment control. On 90 ISD-Agent-Bench scenarios, AlignmentGraph-ISD ranks first among seven agents in two benchmark runs---84.07 with an LLM (runner-up 82.70) and 84.65 with a 9B small language model (runner-up 82.03)---with all pairwise differences significant after Holm correction, and with the margin widening as the model gets smaller. \end{abstract}
