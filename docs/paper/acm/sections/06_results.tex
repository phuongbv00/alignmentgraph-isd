\emph{TN1 and TN2 have not yet been run on the frozen configuration. This section
states what each analysis reports and in what order; every value is marked \TBD{}
and will be filled from the generated artifacts, never typed by hand. No number
from system development enters this section.}

\subsection{Output robustness across the ladder}
\label{sec:robustness}

The first question is whether a system produces a usable design at all, because a
comparison of quality among systems that did not all finish is a comparison among
survivors. We report, per system and size, the proportion of the 90 scenarios
yielding a complete ADDIE output (\Cref{fig:failure-rate}). Failure mechanisms are named per system rather than
pooled, since they have different causes and different implications: a run that
stops at the per-call token cap is a deployment property of that architecture,
while a schema parse failure is not. Values: \TBD.

reported rather than resolved in favour of the more convenient one. Each cell states

\subsubsection{Instrument sensitivity}
\label{sec:sensitivity}

The sensitivity star asks whether the ranking is a property of the outputs or an
artifact of one instrument. The reported quantity is whether the \emph{direction} of
each pairwise comparison survives each arm---not a rank correlation over the full
ordering, which the mid-table systems are too close to resolve and which the paper
never claimed. Encoder-lineage arms, the non-semantic floor, the baseline
correction, and the Bloom arm: \TBD{} (\Cref{tab:rq2-sensitivity}).

\subsection{Ablation (Objective 4)}
\label{sec:ablation}

TN2 separates the two factors of \Cref{tab:ablation-arms} at 0.8B and 4B. The
registered tests are the main effects of decomposition (on the ADDIE score) and of
substrate (on closed triads) at each size and their change with size; the internal
audit metrics---relation coverage, residual violations, stages that gave up---and
the alignment panel describe the arms. Main effects of decomposition and of
substrate, and their interaction: \TBD{} (\Cref{tab:ablation}, \Cref{fig:ablation-components}). The
registered directional prediction, that quality degrades with stage position under
\texttt{single} and less so under \texttt{multi}, widening as the model shrinks:
\TBD. The budget-parity condition---that no arm was cut short by its turn
budget---holds or does not: \TBD; the ablation may be read only if it holds.

Each statement below will name the instrument family it rests on. Where the LLM judge
and the panel disagree about the arms, that disagreement is a finding and is
reported in both readings (\Cref{sec:discussion}), not resolved silently.

\subsection{Expert review}
\label{sec:expert-results}

The blinded expert review is reported descriptively: how reviewers rated
constructive alignment, feasibility and clarity, how much revision each design would
need, and how those ratings relate to the panel. It is corroboration, not a second
endpoint, and it is not used to adjudicate the automated results. \TBD.

\subsection{Cost}
\label{sec:cost}

Cost is reported as a trade-off, not as an efficiency claim. Per-scenario model
calls and token totals per system and size, read from \texttt{token\_usage} only,
appear in \Cref{fig:cost-quality} against quality. Two disclosures accompany every
cost statement: the cost columns for systems that did not complete every scenario
are survivor numbers, so cost comparisons use only systems with full coverage; and
wall-clock time is queued throughput under parallel scheduling, not latency, so it
is not reported as such. Values: \TBD.
