Every generated table pairs a mean with the between-run SD of the same signal.
Cells with fewer than \ladderNumScenarios{} scenarios are marked in table

\subsection{Robustness by Scale}
\label{sec:robustness}

AlignmentGraph-ISD produced usable output for every scenario at every size. The two
least scaffolded agents lost most outputs at the small end, with Holm-corrected
Fisher tests supporting their failure-rate differences from the harness. Their
mechanisms differed: for \costWorstCoverageAgent{} at
\costWorstCoverageSize{}, \costWorstCoverageTruncPct\% of missing artifacts ended
in \texttt{LengthFinishReasonError} at exactly
\texttt{completion\_tokens = 8192}, whereas EduPlanner failures were dominated by
schema parsing. Under the envelope in \Cref{sec:assumptions}, the former is a
deployability result: one-shot generation often cannot finish within the target
call budget, while specialist calls remain below it. The generated
\texttt{stats\_cost.md} reports the full failure and truncation roster. Because
truncation does not explain every failure, score analyses use
\texttt{failure=0} primarily and complete-case only as sensitivity.

The decomposed generate--verify--repair process produces stronger ADDIE artifacts
but a longer, less compact trace than single-loop designs, so Trajectory favors
ReAct-ISD and ADDIE-Agent at every size (\Cref{tab:rq1-ladder-traj}) and the

$\rqOneDeltaBestAddieNineB$ with bootstrap 95\% CI

\rqOneDiDBaselineName{}, the largest-minus-smallest ADDIE DiD is
$\rqOneDiDVsBestBaseline$ (95\% CI
$[\rqOneDiDVsBestBaselineCILo,\rqOneDiDVsBestBaselineCIHi]$,

meaningful inference; that selection is precisely what \texttt{failure=0} avoids.

\subsection{RQ2: Constructive-Alignment Signal Panel}

Under the pre-specified rule, the panel favors AlignmentGraph-ISD at every size: at

\subsection{Ablation: Mechanism Evidence}
\label{sec:ablation}

The ablation is judge-invariant but alignment-shifted: removing graph context
changes little in ADDIE scores but lowers the RQ2 panel broadly. Removing the
verifier from the shipped configuration likewise has no supported score effect,
and no ablation arm fails to produce output.

\input{../../generated/tab_ablation}

Graph context is therefore the supported mechanism for the alignment-signal
profile, whereas robustness is shared by every decomposed arm and cannot be
causally assigned to decomposition without a monolithic control. The verifier
remains a repair backstop and source of audit evidence. When graph context is
absent, its simple effect on \texttt{objective\_assessment\_similarity} is positive
at every size, from \ablVerifCtxOffNineB{} to
\ablVerifCtxOffZeroEightB{}; this counterfactual does not establish a verifier
score effect in the shipped system. Nor does the ablation support a uniform
mechanism-by-scale claim.

\subsection{Sensitivity of the RQ2 Reading}
\label{sec:sensitivity}

The pairwise direction favoring AlignmentGraph-ISD persists across independent
neural-encoder lineages, the TF--IDF lexical floor, the BERT--lexicon Bloom axis,
and the complete-case slice. On the encoder axis it remains first in
\rqTwoSensTopOneCellsEncoderAxis{} arm--size cells and on the Bloom axis in
\rqTwoSensTopOneCellsBloomAxis{} cells.

The comparison is therefore not tied to one encoder, semantic embeddings as a

complete-case result remains sensitivity evidence, not an unconditional analysis.

\subsection{Cost and Throughput}
\label{sec:cost}

The harness uses \costHarnessCallsTwoB{} calls and
\costHarnessTokensTwoB{} thousand tokens per design at 2B, and
\costHarnessCallsFourB{} calls and \costHarnessTokensFourB{} thousand tokens at
4B. Its token use is \costTokenRatioVsMedianBaselineNineB$\times$--
\costTokenRatioVsMedianBaselineZeroEightB$\times$ the median baseline and
\costTokenRatioVsCheapestCompleteZeroEightB$\times$--
\costTokenRatioVsCheapestCompleteTwoB$\times$ ReAct-ISD, the cheapest baseline
with complete coverage.

\input{../../generated/tab_cost}

Tokens per ADDIE point favor the trade-off at the two smaller sizes, deteriorate at
4B, and lose at 9B, where ReAct-ISD reaches parity or better while using
approximately $1/\costTokenRatioVsCheapestCompleteNineB$ of the tokens. Component
telemetry attributes \costVerifierTokenOverheadFourB\%--
\costVerifierTokenOverheadTwoB\% token overhead to the verifier and
\costGraphContextTokenOverheadZeroEightB\%--
\costGraphContextTokenOverheadTwoB\% to graph context, the larger mechanism and the
one supported by the RQ2 ablation.

Baseline and EduPlanner token columns are survivor statistics because truncated
cases leave no artifact; comparisons therefore use full-coverage baselines.
Wall-clock values measure queued throughput with up to
\costMaxInflightDesigns{} designs in flight, not request latency. All token values
come from \texttt{metadata.token\_usage}; inconsistent top-level cost fields are
excluded.
