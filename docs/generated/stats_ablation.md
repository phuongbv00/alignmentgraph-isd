`interaction` = (component ON − OFF given the other ON) − (component ON − OFF given the other OFF). A negative interaction means the two mechanisms substitute for each other ONLY where both simple effects carry the beneficial sign; where both are negative (`trajectory_score`, `total_score`, on which the verifier lowers the score) read it as a magnitude statement — the verifier costs less when graph context is on — and where the two simple effects have opposite signs it is a crossover, neither substitution nor complementarity.

p-values here are uncorrected. Note that the `verifier | ctx ON` and `graphctx | verif ON` columns are the A0-vs-A1 and A0-vs-A2 comparisons, which the sections above report Holm-corrected within their family; only the `| ... OFF` columns and `interaction` are contrasts unique to this section.

Signals in this section are on two scales: `addie_median`, `trajectory_score` and `total_score` are rubric points out of 100; the 7 RQ2 panel signals are on [0,1]. Magnitudes are not comparable across the two groups.
